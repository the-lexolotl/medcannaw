The second half of the week

What’s on, Thursday to Sunday.

Online sessions are booked through Eventbrite, and MCUK CIC’s are free. For in-person events, check the programme page for venue and entry details. All times are UK time.

Thursday 5 November
1pm Vapourisers & Thermal Extraction Devices
Live online demonstration · free  |  MCUK CIC

A practical session on vapourisers and thermal extraction devices, how they’re used with prescribed CBPMs, and what patients should be aware of. Bring your questions.

Book free
6:30pm Living as a Patient: Access & Experience
Online discussion · free  |  MCUK CIC

What it’s really like to navigate life as a CBPM patient, from access and treatment pathways to the everyday challenges beyond the consultation room.

Book free
7:30pm The UPA Story: “Did we actually win?”
Panel discussion  |  United Patients Alliance

The story of UPA and the UK patient campaign, from Parliament to the 2018 law change, then a question for the next generation: what are we fighting for now?

Book now
Friday 6 November
2pm Cosmic Tree + terpene analysis by KAST
Patient meet-up · Gloucester  |  PatientsCann UK

Meet patients and share practical information. KAST will test your medicine, give you a terpene breakdown and explain how to understand it better.

Details
6:30pm The Community
Online roundtable · free  |  MCUK CIC

Patient organisations, clinics, producers and industry on who we are, the challenges we share, and how we can work together with patients at the centre.

Book free
7pm Grow Your Own
Discussion  |  United Patients Alliance

Should medical cannabis patients be allowed to grow their own medicine? Affordability, autonomy, safety, other countries’ models and what legislation could look like.

Book now
Saturday 7 November
1:30pm No. 10 Downing Street awareness action
Petition hand-in + static protest · London  |  PatientsCann UK

A peaceful, visible action taking the patient message to the heart of government. Petition hand-in at 1:30pm, then a static protest from 2pm to 6pm.

Sign the letter
3pm The Future of the Campaign
Panel  |  United Patients Alliance

The UPA finale. Patients, campaigners, doctors and clinics answer one question: what’s the single biggest change patients need in the next five years? UPA then presents its 2027–2030 Campaign Agenda.

Book now
6:30pm The Future
Online panel · free  |  MCUK CIC

Where do we go from here? Developments in treatment, research, access and the wider patient movement, and what we want the years ahead to look like.

Book free
Sunday 8 November
7pm Closing Event: Q&A, Reflections & Looking Ahead
Online webinar + audience Q&A · free  |  MCUK CIC

A final Q&A using the questions raised across the week, reflections on MCAW 2026, and a look at what comes next.

Book free

Can’t be in London on Saturday?

Add your name to the letter to No. 10.

PatientsCann UK’s open letter asks the Prime Minister to stand with prescribed medical cannabis patients and tackle the stigma they face at work, at home and in public. Every name will be handed in at 10 Downing Street on Saturday afternoon.
